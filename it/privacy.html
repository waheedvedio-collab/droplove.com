Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · DrofLove.com

What we collect

We collect only what you give us: email, username, name, birth date, gender, orientation, location, bio, interests, and any photos you upload. We never request bank or ID details.

How we use it

  • To operate the service: matching, discover feeds, messaging.
  • To secure the platform: password hashing, rate limiting, abuse detection.
  • To review safety reports you submit.

We do not sell your data. We do not run targeted advertising. We do not profile you for third parties.

Cookies and sessions

We use one session cookie to keep you signed in, and remember your language preference. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking pixels.

Your rights

  • Access: view all your data on your profile and settings pages.
  • Correction: edit your profile at any time.
  • Deletion: delete your account permanently from settings.

Data retention

Data is retained while your account exists. On account deletion, your personal data and uploaded files are removed permanently. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Security

Passwords are hashed with a modern algorithm. Transfers use HTTPS. Uploaded files are re-encoded and stored outside the web root, served only through a validated endpoint.

Contact

Contact us with any privacy questions or data requests.
